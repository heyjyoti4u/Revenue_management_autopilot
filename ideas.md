# SignalStay — Design Direction

## Reference Ground Truth

The reference is [Otamiser](https://www.otamiser.com/), a premium revenue-management landing page for short-term rentals and independent hotels. Preserve the reference's information architecture: a rounded navigation bar, a strong hero proposition, proof metrics, a rank-led point of view, a connected revenue loop, operational benefit sections, communication-channel support, an included-services catalogue, and a clear consultation CTA. Do not copy the brand, exact wording, logo, or visual identity. The redesign should feel like the same category and business model, but clearly belong to a new product.

## Chosen Direction: Signal Navy / Electric Violet Operations Desk

### Design Movement
Editorial fintech interface meets boutique-hospitality art direction: precise data surfaces, atmospheric photography, and a confident, dark operational canvas rather than a generic SaaS dashboard.

### Core Principles
1. **Signal before noise:** every section should clarify the business signal that precedes bookings, not bury the idea in decoration.
2. **Operational warmth:** pair analytical visual language with human hospitality cues, warm light, tactile imagery, and calm copy.
3. **Asymmetric confidence:** use split layouts, off-axis visual anchors, and intentional empty space instead of stacking everything in centered cards.
4. **Selective intensity:** electric violet is reserved for signals, active states, and key actions; the rest is deep navy, ink, mist, and warm white.

### Color Philosophy
Deep navy (#080D22) creates the feeling of a quiet command center. Electric violet (#8B5CF6) signals live intelligence and movement without copying the reference's acid green. Lilac (#C4B5FD) is used sparingly for explanatory emphasis. Warm white (#F7F5F0) keeps the interface human and editorial, while slate blue-gray supports long-form reading. A small copper accent (#E6A873) appears only in hospitality details and photography overlays, grounding the system in real spaces.

### Layout Paradigm
A long-form editorial landing page organized as a sequence of operational moments. Navigation and hero are full-bleed dark; proof metrics break the canvas with a pale surface; comparison content uses a rail-and-panel composition; the revenue loop becomes a diagonal signal path; benefit sections alternate text and image, with a prominent communication panel and a dense service matrix near the end. Desktop layouts should feel slightly off-center and mobile layouts should collapse to an intentional single-column reading order.

### Signature Elements
- **Signal rail:** thin violet rules, numbered steps, and small live-status labels that connect sections vertically.
- **Orbit mark:** the three-loop brand symbol appears in the header, favicon, hero diagram, and lightweight decorative stamps.
- **Operational surfaces:** translucent navy panels with hairline borders and small mono labels, never generic rounded cards everywhere.

### Interaction Philosophy
Interactions should feel like a confident operator responding to a signal: buttons compress quickly on press, navigation links reveal a thin violet underline, tabs and comparison states switch without drama, and hover states bring data surfaces forward with a small lift. CTAs remain direct and human. Any placeholder action should explain what is coming rather than pretending to complete a workflow.

### Animation
Use short 160–240ms ease-out transitions for controls. On initial load, reveal the hero copy first, then the orbital signal diagram, then proof metrics in a 60ms stagger. Let the signal rail draw subtly from top to bottom as sections enter, but keep motion behind `prefers-reduced-motion`. Do not animate layout dimensions; use transform and opacity only. Avoid looping glows and excessive parallax.

### Typography System
Use **Space Grotesk** for headlines, metric numerals, navigation, and buttons: geometric, assertive, and more ownable than a default SaaS sans. Use **DM Sans** for body copy and long-form explanation. Use **IBM Plex Mono** for labels, metrics metadata, and status chips. Headlines are large, tight, and left-aligned; italic emphasis is reserved for one short phrase per major heading. Body copy remains 16–18px with a 1.6 line-height. All-caps labels are small, letter-spaced, and used as signposts, not decoration.

### Brand Essence
**SignalStay gives hospitality operators the rank, pricing, and distribution signals to run revenue as a daily operation—not a monthly report.**

Personality: **precise, composed, incisive**.

### Brand Voice
Headlines are short, point-of-view-led, and slightly contrarian. CTAs sound like an operator inviting a useful conversation, not a funnel. Microcopy is direct, specific, and quietly confident.

Example headline: “Your calendar is late. Your rank is early.”

Example CTA: “See the signal behind your next booking.”

### Wordmark & Logo
Use the generated three-orbit signal mark without text in the header and favicon. Pair it with the custom wordmark **SignalStay** in Space Grotesk with slightly tightened tracking; the capital S-to-S transition is the recognizable verbal signature. Never substitute a generic logo or text glyph.

### Signature Brand Color
**Electric Violet — #8B5CF6.** It owns the idea of a live signal moving through a dark operational field and differentiates the redesign from Otamiser's green-led identity.

## Content Decisions

Use the reference's business themes while rewriting copy for SignalStay. Keep proof metrics explicitly labelled as illustrative demo figures where appropriate; do not present them as verified customer outcomes. Avoid fabricated customer reviews, star ratings, or testimonials. Use real, inspectable interface motifs—rank movement, pricing windows, channel mix, alerts, and communication summaries—rather than fictional quotes.
